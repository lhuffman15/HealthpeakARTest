Portside Campus · Healthpeak Bay Area

Put the campus on your desk

Drag to look around the buildings. On a phone, place a 3D model of Portside on any table and walk around it. No app needed.

Lands about 60 × 40 cm · 24 × 16 in

Open it on your phone

Scan with your phone camera, then tap View on your desk. Works on iPhone and Android, right in the browser.

  1. Point at a table. Move your phone slowly for a second so it can find the surface.
  2. Place the campus. It sets down at desk size. Drag to move it, twist to turn it.
  3. Pinch to resize. Spread your fingers to stand among the buildings.
